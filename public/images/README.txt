PUT YOUR HOME PAGE TEAM PHOTO HERE
==================================
1. Download your photo (a real team / staff meeting photo, landscape, at least 1600 x 1200 px).
2. Rename it exactly:   hero-team.jpg      (hero-team.jpeg, hero-team.webp or hero-team.png also work)
3. Copy it into THIS folder (public/images/).
4. Stop the server (Ctrl + C) and run:  npm run dev   again.  The home page now shows your photo.

To go back to the animated illustration, just delete or rename the photo.

LICENCE: check the licence on the download page. Many free photos require you to credit the
author. If so, type the credit in src/lib/site.ts -> heroPhotoCredit and it appears in the footer.
